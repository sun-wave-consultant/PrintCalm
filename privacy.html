Privacy Policy

Provider: Sun Wave Consultant LLC
Effective date: November 20, 2026

Sun Wave Consultant LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides PrintCalm, a photo sizing, layout, and printing app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, purchase PrintCalm Pro, or contact us.

1. Photos and information processed on your device

You can import images using the system photo picker, Files, or the clipboard. On supported iPhones, you can also take a photo for an ID photo project after granting camera permission. PrintCalm uses the images you select to size, crop, rotate, arrange, preview, print, and export photos and ID photo layouts.

Copies of imported images, pages, drafts, editing settings, and preferences (such as language and measurement units) are stored locally on your device. PrintCalm does not modify your original photos. When you choose a file, the app may read its name, size, creation and modification dates, and image metadata to identify, validate, or display it.

ID photo framing and background adjustment use on-device image processing. ID photos and facial images may be sensitive. PrintCalm does not send facial images to us or use them for identity verification, biometric profiling, or advertising. Only use photos you have permission to use.

PrintCalm does not require an account. The current version does not send photos, projects, device identifiers, or usage history to us and does not include advertising tracking or third-party analytics.

2. Camera, Photos, printing, and sharing

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo using the supported ID photo camera feature. You can change camera permissions in system settings or import images without using the camera. The system photo picker provides the app with the items you select.

When you choose to print, export, save, or share, the printer, system service, storage provider, app, or recipient you select may receive the file needed to complete your action. These third parties, cloud drives, and backup services handle files under their own policies and your settings. PrintCalm does not independently upload your photos to a remote printing service.

3. PrintCalm Pro purchases

PrintCalm Pro is an optional, one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Apple processes purchases through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not directly receive your payment card details or billing credentials.

The app uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to unlock Pro features, restore eligible purchases, and recognize refunded or revoked purchases. Apple processes purchase-related information under Apple’s Privacy Policy. PrintCalm does not require a separate account with us for purchases.

4. Contacting support

If you contact us, we may receive your email address, any name you provide, your message, and attachments you choose to send. We use these details to respond, troubleshoot, and address requests or legal obligations. We retain support correspondence only as reasonably needed for these purposes or as required by law. Please avoid sending unredacted identity documents, identification numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

We do not sell personal information or use support correspondence for targeted advertising.

5. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Pages and ID photo drafts remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove associated app data. You can view, edit, export, and delete supported local projects. Exported files, copies in other apps, cloud storage, and backups must be managed separately. Because we do not receive your local photos or projects, we generally cannot retrieve or delete them remotely.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, or exercise other applicable privacy rights. Contact us below; we may reasonably verify requests where appropriate.

6. Children

PrintCalm is a general-purpose photo tool and is not specifically directed to children. Parents and guardians should supervise or authorize use of children’s photographs where required by law. Contact us if you have a privacy concern involving a child.

7. Security

PrintCalm is designed to keep photos and drafts on your device. Protection of local information also depends on your device security, operating system, backups, and services you choose. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or applicable requirements change. We will update the effective date above and provide additional notice or seek consent where required by law.
