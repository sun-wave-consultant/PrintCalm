PrintCalm Support

PrintCalm helps you size, arrange, preview, print, and export photos on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. No PrintCalm account is required.

Contact support

For help, bug reports, or feedback, email support@sun-wave.xyz.

To help us investigate, please include your device model, operating system version, PrintCalm app version, the steps that led to the issue, and any error message. Screenshots are optional. Please do not send unredacted identity documents or sensitive personal photos.

Frequently asked questions

How do I print at the exact size I entered?

Choose the correct paper size in PrintCalm and in your printer settings. Turn off print scaling such as “Fit to Page” when appropriate. Printer margins, borderless support, and drivers can affect the final size. For important prints, make a test print and measure it with a ruler.

Can I export without a printer?

Yes. You can export a PDF or JPG and print it later or use a printing service. Confirm that the service does not resize the output.

How do I arrange multiple photos on one sheet?

Import your images, choose the paper dimensions, and use the available layout or arrangement controls. You can adjust placement and cropping before previewing the sheet.

Can I make passport or ID photos?

PrintCalm provides ID photo size presets and custom dimensions. Check the receiving authority’s latest rules for size, framing, background, expression, and image quality. PrintCalm cannot guarantee acceptance.

What is included for free?

You can edit, save drafts, and repeatedly print or export eligible layouts without watermarks. Free output supports up to four photos per sheet, including supported ID photo sheets, and a single ID photo JPG.

What does PrintCalm Pro unlock?

PrintCalm Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks output of five or more photos per sheet, expanded ID photo sheet output, and supported specialized white-border layouts. Available layouts depend on your paper and photo sizes.

How do I restore PrintCalm Pro?

Open PrintCalm’s Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account associated with the original purchase. If restoration fails, confirm your account and internet connection, then contact support. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.

How can I request a refund?

For App Store purchases, visit Apple’s Report a Problem website and follow Apple’s instructions. Refund decisions are made by Apple.

Where are my photos and drafts stored?

PrintCalm processes selected photos and stores its working copies and drafts on your device. Exported files, cloud-drive copies, and backups are managed separately by the relevant service. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does PrintCalm modify my original photos?

No. Imported source photos are kept intact; editing works with app-managed copies and project data.
